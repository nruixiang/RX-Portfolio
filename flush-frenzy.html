Overview

Game Overview

Flush Frenzy is a 4-player physical card game. Each player competes to collect cleaning supplies to keep their toilet clean by managing dice rolls and strategising card combinations. The core loop revolves around rolling coloured dice and making decisions to maximise the chance that the dice rolls match cards in your hand.

Process

Project Process

Due to the extensive complexity of the game, it was very difficult for new players to comprehend the rules. I went through several iterations of phrasing the instructions, alongside introducing new mechanics that elevated the overall game experience while not overwhelming first-time players.

Challenges

Key Challenges

The greatest challenge was by far phrasing the rules in a way first-time players could understand. Initially the game had no special actions. Introducing them revealed through playtesting that new players struggled to grasp all the actions at once, which severely limited their decision-making.

We also found that having someone actively demonstrate the actions and combinations vastly accelerated learning. This insight drove the creation of reference cards — dedicated visual aids designed to convey card combo functions at a glance, even within tight size constraints.

Deliverables

Rulebook Creation

I created two distinct rulebook layouts — one designed to resemble a ply of toilet paper (thematically on-brand), and one following a standard page format. The final product was a hybrid of the two approaches.
