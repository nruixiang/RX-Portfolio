Background

Driven by
curiosity

I'm a Game Designer with six years of project experience, having designed and developed multiple games across different scopes and genres. My primary focus is mechanical and technical design — ensuring systems are balanced, scalable, and satisfying to interact with.

Beyond development, I've worked on visual and media design during my time as part of my army's media staff, creating posters and promotional materials.

I adapt quickly, experiment often, and enjoy diving deep into both the creative and technical sides of development. I see feedback not as criticism, but as fuel for growth — an opportunity to sharpen ideas and build stronger systems.

As a lifelong gamer, I'm especially drawn to MMOs, farming simulators, and shooters — games that give players room to experiment and shape their own experience.
