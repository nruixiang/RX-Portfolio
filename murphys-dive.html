Overview

Game Overview

Murphy's Dive is a Dungeon-Crawler Action Shooter. It features Murphy, an aspiring adventurer with grand ambitions delving into a dungeon to recover a legendary sword. Tragedy strikes as his party fall along the way, but Murphy seizes the chance to claim their weapons. However, due to his lack of skill, he is unable to wield them properly. Will he succeed in claiming the legendary sword or fall in his attempt?

Inspired by Vampire Survivors and Enter the Gungeon, the core twist is weapons that interact with the player in unexpected ways — adding an extra layer of challenge and skill expression beyond typical shooters.

Process

Project Process

The ideation stage began with consulting the team on what genre to develop. We came up with several possible genres and voted — the majority chose a Vampire Survivors / Enter the Gungeon-style game.

I proposed adding an additional layer to weapons — having them interact in an expected yet unexpected manner with the player. The team liked this idea, so we proceeded with coming up with fun concepts to implement.

The artist proposed a comedic story that fell in line with our core mechanic, and also set the art direction the game would follow.

Challenges

Development Challenges

I encountered several difficulties during prototyping, as I had to develop the prototype from scratch on my own. I had underestimated the scale of our idea, which required cutting certain features to meet deadlines.

  • 1

    Unstable System Foundation

    Exploration required free backtracking. My initial code only supported one entry and exit point, meaning only a single path could exist. I had to refactor the entire system to support multiple routes, which took considerable time.

  • 2

    Unfamiliarity with Sprite Animation

    For smooth gameplay, good sprite animation is essential. In past projects I left this to others while focusing on mechanics. Since I'd implemented certain behaviours before receiving assets, I had to refactor everything that ran with animation to ensure smooth sprite transitions.

Level Design

Level Design

Murphy's Dive was designed to feel similar to Binding of Isaac, but with a twist. The core idea revolves around weapons that hurt the player — drawbacks the player must work with to succeed. For example, an explosive homing crossbow that tracks the player instead of enemies.

Level design focuses on using walls as a means to elevate these quirks, requiring players to utilise the environment to get the most out of their weapons.

This room was designed to familiarise the player with a weapon whose projectile ricochets off walls. Simple walls with minor edges create unpredictability, increasing the likelihood players accidentally hit themselves — reinforcing the core weapon mechanic.

This room was designed to give a flying ranged enemy an advantage against the player. Projectiles can fly freely through gaps in the ground, the player has less room to dodge, and must approach the enemy to have more space to move.
