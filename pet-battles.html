Project Overview

Pet Battles is a pet collection auto-battler game concept developed on Figma. The player collects pets by attracting them using furniture obtained via gacha. The pets are then used to form teams to battle other players — the outcome determined by who successfully drafted the better team composition.

I was responsible for designing and prototyping the Gacha and Combat features, assisting with AI-generated GIF assets, creating animations to enhance look and feel, and contributing wherever else needed.
